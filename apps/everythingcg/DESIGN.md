# EverythingCG

App layer for everythingcg.codesavory.dev, a curated CG news river with jobs, tools, learning, design and art. It runs on the core roles. It adds no tokens.

## What the Ring measures

Freshness. Each item carries a small dot that is the accent when the story is under 24 hours old and neutral after that. It is the only use of the accent in the river, so the eye lands on what is new.

## Screens and the one spark

- Home: the river, newest first, filtered by domain. Spark: the freshness dot on stories under 24 hours.
- Jobs: link-outs to studio boards. Spark: the studio count.
- Tools, Learn, Library, Picks: quiet lists. No spark.
- Support: the Buy Me a Coffee button is the page's single accent.

## Rules for this product

- Link out. Headline, a short excerpt for sources that allow it, and the original link. Red tier sources show a headline and a link only.
- Every item names its source. Machine text is never shown as the editor's voice.
- No tracking, no ads, no third-party scripts. Fonts and images are self-hosted. YouTube is a link, not an embed.
- Affiliate links, when they exist, are labelled "paid link" next to the link, with a plain publisher link beside it.
- Copy is terse. No em dashes, en dashes or double hyphens.
- Wall notes come in one warm-gray paper family (paper, fog, stone, sand, clay) plus one ember wash. No pastels. Retired colours render as their monochrome kin.
- Icon studio marks are monochrome by default; the ember tile is a tonal same-hue gradient, never multi-hue. Downloads carry only the mark.
